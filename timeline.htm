History of Costumes by Matrices

From newest to oldest, this is a timeline of all the costumes I've built or had a major part in building. Note: Some collaborations with colleagues and refurbished pieces are not pictured (for example if it was a minor change intended to match the original maker's work, or if a colleague used my foam base to finish into a costume collaboration in their style).
 

Ace (July 2026) - Palamute partial fursuit. Collaboration with DekaWolf that we worked on together!

Matrices (Head & tail finished Aug 2021, hand and footpaws finished July 2026) - Partial fursuit to be worn with clothing.

Chelan (Updated July 2021) - New nose, shave, airbrushing touch up. As well as new padding on the interior. Her new nose is by Mythimorph Labs .

Growlithe (Jan 2020) - Cosplay character partial. The shirt and coveralls were also edited to go with this cosplay character.

Costa (Dec 2019) - Fullsuit & head refurbish. Body, feet, and tail by me. Original maker of head and handpaws by Soul Enterprise. Collie Mutt Dog.
