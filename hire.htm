Matrices' Creative Services

Thank you for considering me for your project! I am very excited to have the opportunity to create wonderful things for clients. I am a freelance artist, tailor, and skilled crafter. My expertise lies in working with the mediums of faux fur and foam to create high quality luxury wearable costumes, patterned plush sculptures, and props. I offer unique and useful costume parts, accessories, commission consulting to talk about items clients would like custom made, and I am available to inspect items slated to be refurbished. I offer a guarantee on my sewing, check out my terms for more details on working with me.

For additional details on the work I do, check out the categories for the creative service you are interested in. My commission openings depend on the workload I have in my project queue. It is my goal to keep a short queue and only to accept a few commissions at a time to provide high quality workmanship and full attention to detail to clients who are interested in my custom character illustrations, handcrafted costume parts, prop commissions, sewn projects, or my refurbishing and tailoring services as well as my tutoring and teaching opportunities!
