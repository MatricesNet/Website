Matrices' Portfolio

          Hello and welcome! My name is Sara Howard, also known as Matrices. I am an artist, tailor, illustrator, and crafter. The following is a portfolio of my work and examples of my products that I make and sell. More examples and the most up-to-date place to find all examples of my work can be found at my Trello Queue, I have 5-star rated customer service as seen in my Etsy reviews. Additionally you can find additional selected examples of my work at my FurAffinity Gallery and Weasyl Gallery.

My Goals

  • Helping other people learn how to create and enhance their own costumes, backed up by my site with over 80 tutorials and articles on the topic.
  • Providing patterns and items that enhance existing or future costumes.
  • Tailoring and refurbishing, a per-hour service I offer to perform onsite or as take-home work.
  • Commission consulting, to talk about items clients would like custom made.
  • I am an illustrator offering fun cartoony merchandise and commission opportunities.
  • Offering unique and useful costume parts and accessories that I have created.

          I offer a 3 month guarantee on my sewing. I invite potential customers to try on my merchandise, and I will do exchanges/returns on unworn items during the event if something does not fit.

Patterns and Supplies

To meet with my goals of helping other people learn to create, and to suppliment the over 80 fursuit building tutorials available free on my website, I also make available patterns and premium tutorials for sale that are of my design. These patterns are geared to people new to sewing, or want to get into making. They are sold with instructions, a full-size copy of the pattern, and are packaged in Matrices.net packaging.

All of my patterns come with an instructional booklet, they are also available for purchase in my etsy shop, but at events I make them available as a physical copy for those without printing or that prefer the included booklet. It is the packaged pattern with printed and bound booklet. I do all the printing and binding myself for these.

When available, I will also package some patterns with enough material big enough to practice and/or create the patterned project or provide the pieces pre-cut in a kit. An example of a Foam Ear kit for fursuit head building, with thourough instructions printed in a tutorial format for use.
