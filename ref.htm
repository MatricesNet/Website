While I do my best to accomodate everyone, please be aware there are times where I may need to work through paid work before I can take on additional clients. Ask for availibility! The following are examples of how my character reference sheet commission opportunities break down.

Character reference sheets illustrated by me are a custom-made personalized experience. They are more involved than a simple illustration. I can work from a variety of resources such as photos, sketches, or written concepts, or even artistic liberty (my design) with your color and species prompt. I will do my best to meet your goals and the character you would like portrayed. It is my goal to get your vision understood and drawn as a unique character, just for you!

The use of a reference sheet is so other artists are able to interperet your character visually. It speeds up the commission process for future art and is required for many other artistic endeavors involving your custom character. Its also just fun to see and show your friends. Reference sheets are primarily unclothed and done in flat colors, so other artists can understand your character's markings and shape. They are treated as color-accurate so they do not have light effects (like shading) so markings are not ambiguous. I do offer detail shots which can have painted and textured features as well.

Some of the details your ref sheet can include are

  • Full Body Views - $40 each flat color.
    • Front.
    • Side.
    • Three Quarter.
    • Back.
  • Headshot Views - $25 each flat color, $35 each for painted.
    • Facial Expessions.
    • Hairstyle Options.
    • Top Down or alternate views.
    • Showing off different accessories (with glasses, hats, etc).
  •  Additional Views - Prices vary, depends on the level of detail needed.
    • "Focused" areas, such as a specific marking or detail.
    • Up-close views of hands or feet.
    • Featured Accessories.
    • Props.
  • Other Details - Included!
    • Color Swatches.
    • Name, gender symbol, text details.
    • Solid background color or simple gradient.
