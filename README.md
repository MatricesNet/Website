# Matrices' Website
Welcome! This is the personal homepage of Sara "Matrices" Howard. A costume designer and cartoony artist, who has been providing art and fursuit tutorials for the furry fandom for many years. The primary goal of this website is to provide work in progress photos and step-by-step guidance to anyone of any skill level who is also interested in creating their own fursuit-style costume. Other goals are to show off personal characters, costumes, art, special interest projects, and other various services that Matrices provides. 
## How To Get In Touch
Matrices has a contact page [available here](https://www.matrices.net/contact.htm). Email is best for almost everything, but you can still reach me other ways if you want to get in touch over various formats. If you choose to say hi, it is also best to state your whole comment/question that you might have in the same message/or at least at the same time of initial contact. It is OK to ask questions, I may not be very fast at replying but I can reply to you faster to get you your answer that way! :D
